\section{Supervised CAMELYON16 / PCam (upper bounds)}
Bejnordi et al.\ (JAMA 2017): best CAMELYON16 algorithm slide-level AUC 0.994 (Harvard/MIT, GoogLeNet), lesion FROC 0.807,
outperforming pathologists under time constraint. PatchCamelyon (Veeling et al.\ 2018, 96$\times$96 patches): rotation-
equivariant CNN AUC $\approx$0.96. Attention MIL / CLAM / TransMIL with foundation-model features (UNI, CTransPath):
slide AUC $\approx$0.93--0.99 with only slide labels.

\section{Detection / instance segmentation}
YOLOv8 (Ultralytics 2023) and YOLO11 (2024): real-time one-stage detectors with segmentation heads; YOLO11 reports
higher COCO mAP with $\sim$22\% fewer parameters than YOLOv8 at the m scale. Pathology use: mitosis detection (MIDOG),
nuclei (HoVer-Net, StarDist and YOLO variants), cell counting. Aerial use: vehicles (Potsdam cars).
